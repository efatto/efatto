Keep incoming emails that Odoo cannot route to any record: emails sent
to an unknown alias, a retired mailbox or (optionally) the catch-all
address.

Each email is stored in *Lost Mail > Inbox* with its sender, recipients,
body, original headers and attachments. From the inbox a user can attach
the email to any record with a chatter, attach it to the sender's
contact (creating it if needed) or create a new record, without copying
the message. Automatic routing rules can also handle the email as soon
as it arrives.

Emails that Odoo can route itself (replies, known aliases, a configured
fallback model) are left untouched.

Configuration
=============

To configure the module, go to *Lost Mail > Configuration*:

-  *Settings*: choose the group notified when an email lands in the
   inbox and no rule handles it, whether to keep catch-all mail, whether
   to keep automatic and bulk mail, and the retention delays. The
   company of a lost email is taken from the mail alias domain of the
   recipient.
-  *Routing Rules*: create rules matching the sender, the recipients or
   the subject. A rule can attach the email to the sender's contact,
   attach it to a fixed record, discard it or assign it to a user.
   Contact creation is off by default because the sender address comes
   from the email headers.

The incoming mail server must hand over the messages Odoo cannot route
(an incoming mail server or the mail gateway script, without a
restrictive *Create a New Record* model). The module acts when Odoo
finds no route; it does not read the mailbox by itself.

Usage
=====

When an email cannot be routed, it appears in *Lost Mail > Inbox*:

1. Open the email to read the message, the original headers and the
   attachments.
2. Click *Route* and choose an existing record, the sender's contact or
   a new record of a chatter-enabled model.
3. Optionally assign the email to a user or discard it.

The original message is moved to the chosen record with its author,
date, Message-Id and attachments, so replies to it reach the record as
usual.

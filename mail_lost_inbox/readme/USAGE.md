When an email cannot be routed, it appears in *Lost Mail \> Inbox*:

1.  Open the email to read the message, the original headers and the
    attachments.
2.  Click *Route* and choose an existing record, the sender's contact or
    a new record of a chatter-enabled model.
3.  Optionally assign the email to a user or discard it.

The original message is moved to the chosen record with its author,
date, Message-Id and attachments, so replies to it reach the record as
usual.
